# 3D - DASH
#### A remastering of hit 2013 game ‘Geometry Dash’ in a 3D medium

3D-Dash is a remaster of the 2013 side-scrolling platformer known as ‘Geometry Dash’ created by Robert ‘RobTop’ Topala. The original game features a square shaped player traversing through a series of levels littered with various obstacles in order to reach an end goal and switch to the next level.

Similar to the design of the game’s inspiration, 3D-Dash will feature a cuboid individual that progresses linearly through a series of levels through the act of platforming by various means. However, its variance comes from it’s application of the 3D medium rather than the original game’s 2D environment. While both games feature the player being locked into a constant forward momentum in order to maintain the pace and music of the game, this recreation will apply the 3rd dimension to the player character, thus requiring individuals to consider the game on a deeper level and with more forethought to an action at any given moment.

With various mechanics to leverage the environment, including a five-lane switching system, jumping, gravity changes and other means of traversal, players will need to think critically about situations as they arise in order to successfully traverse the environment without colliding with obstacles such as walls that otherwise forcefully restart the level.
